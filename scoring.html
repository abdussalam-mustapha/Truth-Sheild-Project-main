How We Score the Truth

Every message gets a score from 0% (Totally Fake) to 100% (Verified Truth). Let’s see how our super-smart shield figures it out!

Source Reliability

We check if the info comes from a known, trustworthy place, like a big news site or an official page.

Example: Wikipedia

Emotion Check

Fake news uses big, scary words to make you click. We look for overly dramatic language.

“YOU WON’T BELIEVE THIS!”

Image Check

We scan photos to see if they’ve been photoshopped or taken from somewhere else entirely.
